## 👋 About Us

We are **Group 10** in *37005 Fundamentals of Derivative Security Pricing* at UTS. This organisation holds our shared code, data and reports for the subject's group assessment.

Our purpose is to:

- 🤝 **Collaborate** on one version-controlled codebase instead of passing files around
- 🔬 **Reproduce**: anyone in the group can rerun every number and figure from the raw data
- 📚 **Learn** quantitative finance by building models ourselves, not just reading about them

---

## 🧠 What We Work On

Using real end-of-day option quotes on the **S&P 500**, **Amazon** and **Moderna** from 11 August 2026, we:

| | Topic | Techniques |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 📉 | **Implied term structures** | Extracting discount factors $B(0,T)$ and dividend factors $D(0,T)$ from put/call parity with least squares |
| 🌪️ | **Implied volatility** | Inverting Black–Scholes on at-the-forward options |
| 🔀 | **Exotic options** | Pricing an index outperformance option with a change of numeraire, as a function of correlation |
| 🌳 | **American options** | 1000-step binomial lattices to remove early-exercise premiums, solved by fixed-point iteration |
